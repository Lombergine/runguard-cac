# Positioning — what wins, and how RunGuard is pitched against it

Written 1:30 am, Oct 26. Judged by the Member's office, one winner per district.
Top Apps are then chosen nationally "for technical sophistication, creativity,
and potential to solve real problems."

## What won the four San Jose districts in 2025

| District | App | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| CA-16 Liccardo | Relink | AI job agent for formerly incarcerated people; team of three; community impact |
| CA-17 Khanna | Rosetta | AI language companion; grandmother-at-a-pharmacy origin story |
| CA-18 Lofgren | FCHome | Shelters, clinics, transit passes for homeless youth; born on a crisis line |
| CA-19 Panetta | LeftoSense | AI food-safety check plus donation map; national West runner-up |

2024: WildfireWatch (CA-17), Coursify (CA-18). National 2025 winner: BoostT1D,
a Type 1 diabetes insulin helper with a peer-mentor community. Health apps win.
A runner's injury app (Skeletal Sanctuary) won IL-4 in 2025.

The pattern in every winner: a named community, a personal origin, one
sentence of impact a non-engineer repeats, and "AI" somewhere in the pitch.
What none of them show: evidence that their own numbers are right.

## Where RunGuard stands

Strengths no 2025 winner had:
- A **shipped two-sided product**: athlete app plus coach dashboard, real Apple
  Health data, a real team (Leland XC).
- **Verification**: 29 tests against a published Python package, to nine decimals.
- **The honest innovation**: the only running app that audits its own score and
  tells the athlete how much to trust it. Judges read hundreds of "AI-powered"
  pitches; "it tests itself" is a sentence they have not heard.
- **Technical depth** for Top Apps: EWMA load modelling, bootstrap statistics,
  HealthKit running dynamics, SQLite/Postgres, a native app and a web app.

Gaps against the winners, and the fix tonight:
- **Impact sentence.** Winners lead with who is helped. Lead with: *28 to 38%
  of high-school runners are injured every season; RunGuard tells a runner and
  their coach when the load says it's coming, and how much to trust that.*
- **Personal origin.** Say the real one, in one sentence, on camera. [Aditya.]
- **Named community.** Leland XC, N athletes, one coach. [Aditya: the number.]
- **The word "AI".** The base product has an AI coach chat; 2.0 is the
  *trustworthy* layer under any AI: say "AI that shows its work" rather than
  avoiding the word.
- **Visible on the coach side.** Done tonight: the same audit panels and the
  personal line render on the coach's athlete page.

## Honest odds

Nobody can promise a district win: one office, one judge panel, undisclosed
criteria, and a strong field in all four San Jose districts (Monta Vista,
Irvington, Silver Creek, Evergreen Valley). What is true: RunGuard is a more
complete product than any 2025 winner in these districts, its central claim is
verified rather than asserted, and its pitch is one no one else will make.
Whether that beats a well-told AI story depends on the video. Make the first
twenty seconds about the runners, not the statistics.
